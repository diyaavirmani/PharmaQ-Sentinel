# Five To Seven Minute Demo Script

All records in this script are fictional demonstration data.

## 1. Empty Workspace

- Action: Open `http://localhost:5173`.
- Screen: Locked Complaint Workspace.
- Say: "A draft is created invisibly, and the form is read-only from the first screen."
- Expected: Empty form, assistant, upload area, and extraction progress are visible.
- Fallback: If backend is unavailable, show the compact backend banner.
- Why it matters: Complaint facts enter through controlled workflows, not manual field edits.

## 2. Document Upload

- Action: Upload the fictional Amoxicillin complaint PDF generated by `python -m app.utilities.generate_demo_documents`.
- Screen: Assistant upload area and extraction progress.
- Say: "The uploaded source is preserved, parsed, and used to populate the same read-only form."
- Expected: Apollo Pharmacy, Amoxicillin Capsules 500 mg, `AMX240602`, March 2026, February 2028, Major draft severity, and follow-up questions.
- Fallback: Paste the same complaint text into the assistant if local file upload is unavailable.
- Why it matters: Source evidence stays attached to each populated field.

## 3. Natural-Language Correction

- Action: Send: `Sorry, the batch is BMX240602 and the affected quantity is 48 capsules.`
- Screen: Assistant composer and updated form.
- Say: "Only the requested fields change; unrelated fields stay intact."
- Expected: Batch and quantity update, audit events are recorded, and changed fields highlight.
- Fallback: Use development patch only in local development and explain it is not the production correction path.
- Why it matters: Patch-and-merge semantics prevent silent data loss.

## 4. Evidence Lock

- Action: Open evidence for Batch/Lot Number.
- Screen: Overlay evidence drawer.
- Say: "The original PDF value, user correction, active value, timestamps, and excerpts remain reviewable."
- Expected: Active and previous evidence are shown without changing workspace columns.
- Why it matters: Reviewers can reconstruct why the value changed.

## 5. Batch Blast-Radius

- Action: Open Quality Intelligence, Batch Intelligence, then run analysis and simulation.
- Screen: Batch graph and containment modal.
- Say: "The graph connects PL-04, DEV-2026-023, linked CAPA, related batches, shared packaging lot, distribution, and inventory."
- Expected: `BMX240602`, `BMX240603`, `BMX240604`, Delhi, Mumbai, Jaipur, and simulation-only language.
- Why it matters: QA can see possible blast radius without treating it as a final decision.

## 6. Quality War Room

- Action: Open Quality War Room and run it.
- Screen: Specialist cards, auditor challenge, consensus.
- Say: "Specialist agents propose draft findings, and the compliance auditor rejects unsupported final-cause language."
- Expected: QA, Manufacturing, Packaging/Supplier, Pharmacovigilance, and Compliance Auditor outputs. Auditor rejects any claim that packaging seal failure is confirmed root cause without approved evidence.
- Why it matters: AI suggestions are challenged before review.

## 7. New Safety Information

- Action: Send: `One customer developed swelling after consuming a capsule.`
- Screen: Assistant and risk/safety fields.
- Say: "The system treats this as a possible adverse-event signal and asks focused follow-up questions. It does not diagnose or decide reportability."
- Expected: Safety route includes pharmacovigilance review language.
- Fallback: Use deterministic fixture behavior if live OpenAI is unavailable.
- Why it matters: Safety routing is cautious and human-reviewed.

## 8. Duplicates And Playbook

- Action: Open Investigation Support and run support.
- Screen: Duplicate summary and investigation playbook.
- Say: "The system surfaces recurrence candidates, hypotheses, checks, and CAPA considerations as draft support."
- Expected: Similar complaints and discolouration-focused playbook appear below the workspace.
- Why it matters: Investigation planning stays evidence-grounded.

## 9. Save Complaint

- Action: Click Save Complaint, enter `Demo QA User`, acknowledge non-critical missing information, and save.
- Screen: Save modal, then committed status.
- Say: "Saving creates an immutable complaint version and locks the draft."
- Expected: Complaint number appears and QMS Ledger link is available.
- Why it matters: The ledger is generated from reviewed state.

## 10. Inspector Replay

- Action: Open Evidence & Audit.
- Screen: Inspector Replay.
- Say: "The timeline shows how the record evolved."
- Expected: Field changes, tool names, actors, and timestamps.

## 11. Inspection Brief

- Action: Preview, download PDF, and copy summary from Evidence & Audit.
- Screen: Inspection brief overlay.
- Say: "The brief is generated from the saved version snapshot and linked append-only records."
- Expected: Disclaimer, evidence, audit timeline, checksums, model metadata, limitations, and no internal storage paths.
